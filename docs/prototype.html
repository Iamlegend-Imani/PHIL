Six connected views

The app follows the conversation, not the database.

01 / OVERVIEW

Start with the person.

Establish the patient's goal and the product boundary before anything is summarized.

02 / CONTEXT MAP

Connect the fragments.

Biology, behavior, lived context, care, and source provenance remain visible together.

03 / HEALTH TIMELINE

Sequence is not causation.

Events are placed in time to support inquiry without manufacturing a clinical conclusion.

04 / VISIT BRIEF

The patient chooses.

Priorities and questions are selected for the conversation instead of generated as treatment direction.

05 / HUMAN REVIEW

Accountability stays human.

Incomplete verification blocks approval before the brief can be shared.

06 / METHOD & SAFETY

Limits remain visible.

No diagnosis, treatment advice, invented outcomes, or hidden clinical claims.
