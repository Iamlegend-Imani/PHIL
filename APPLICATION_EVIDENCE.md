# Application Evidence

## Short project answer

My most compelling health technology project is PHIL, a Personal Health Intelligence Layer that I designed and built to explore how fragmented health information could become a clearer, patient-controlled clinical conversation. The working prototype follows a fictional patient through a Context Map, Health Timeline, editable Visit Preparation Brief, and human verification gate. Every input retains its source and uncertainty; patterns are framed as questions rather than diagnoses; and the brief cannot be approved until a person verifies the content. I deliberately removed supplement recommendations and generic protocols from an earlier concept because the product was making claims the evidence had not earned. PHIL is not clinically validated, and I do not present it as one. It is evidence of how I work: I see a system, narrow the need, make the idea tangible, build safety into the architecture, and state clearly what remains unknown.

Live prototype: <https://phil-health-map.imani-kirika116.chatgpt.site>

## One-sentence version

I designed and built PHIL, a patient-controlled health-context prototype that turns fragmented information into a source-aware visit brief while keeping diagnosis, treatment, and final judgment explicitly human-owned.

## Evidence that can be truthfully cited

- Public, functional six-view prototype
- Context Map and source ledger
- Health Timeline with visible uncertainty boundary
- Editable, patient-controlled Visit Preparation Brief
- Human verification gate and versioned decision record
- Published methodology, safety boundaries, fictional scenario, case study, roadmap, and practitioner review protocol
- No fabricated users, outcomes, endorsements, or validation metrics

## Positioning note

PHIL should be presented as evidence of my ability to observe, synthesize, prototype, and revise responsibly. It should not be presented as a fixed solution that I expect a fellowship or clinical partner to adopt.

The strongest framing is:

> I built this to make my thinking testable. I am applying because I want to learn how to begin with rigorous clinical immersion, discover unmet needs with the people inside the system, and earn the right to decide what should be built.

## Relationship to AbundanceApp

[AbundanceApp](https://github.com/Iamlegend-Imani/AbundanceApp) shows the broader thread in my work: translating complex human context into a usable interface. PHIL is the sharper health technology expression of that thread. Together, the projects show continuity without pretending that either is a validated clinical product.
